Summary

The manuscript revisits the Fermi–Pasta–Ulam problem with sustained clarity. The argument is staged in seven sections with working numerical experiments, and the supplementary code is unusually generous — a virtue the field too often forgets. Three matters should be settled before submission.

Strengths

  • Section 3's derivation is the clearest exposition of the soliton-mode argument in recent literature.
  • End-to-end reproducible numerical experiments with published integrator settings.
  • The KAM discussion is appropriately restrained.

Weaknesses

  • Framing of "recurrence" is ambiguous between Sections 2 and 5 — finite-size artefact vs. physical recurrence. One paragraph in the introduction would close this.
  • Figure 4 is unreadable at print scale. Four traces collapse to one stroke; split into 2×2 panels or thin the lines.
  • Thin engagement with related work. Chen & Loro 2024 and al-Hamadi 2025 speak directly to this regime.

Suggested revisions

Begin with the framing paragraph — short, mechanical work. Replot Figure 4 in the same sitting. Set aside a half-day for the literature discussion. The conclusion needs one sentence on what would falsify the reading.
