Summary

The manuscript revisits the Fermi–Pasta–Ulam problem with clarity and unusually generous supplementary code. Three matters should be addressed before submission to a tier-one journal.

Strengths

  • Section 3's derivation is the clearest exposition of the soliton-mode argument in recent literature.
  • End-to-end reproducible numerical experiments with published integrator settings.
  • The KAM discussion is appropriately restrained.

Weaknesses

  • Framing of "recurrence" is ambiguous between Sections 2 and 5 — finite-size artefact vs. physical recurrence. One clarifying paragraph in the introduction would close this.

  • Thin engagement with related work. Chen & Loro 2024 and al-Hamadi 2025 speak directly to this regime.

Suggested revisions
